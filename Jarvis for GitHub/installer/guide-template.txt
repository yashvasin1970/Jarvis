JARVIS - QUICK GUIDE
====================

START / OPEN
  Jarvis starts by itself in the background (no window) about 20 seconds
  after you sign in to Windows. To chat, double-click the "Jarvis" icon on
  your Desktop (or in this folder). Jarvis opens in its own app window, like
  a normal program. You can also use http://127.0.0.1:{{PORT}} in any browser.
  If Jarvis isn't running, the "Jarvis" icon starts it (takes about a minute;
  a few minutes if it is repairing itself after an update).

  Jarvis always opens on a new, empty chat. Your earlier chats are in the
  list on the left; click one to carry on with it.

  Pin it: right-click the "Jarvis" icon on your Desktop > "Show more options"
  > "Pin to taskbar" and/or "Pin to Start".

  The first time the chat opens it may show a "Win a Mac mini" box. Close it
  with the X in its top-right corner. (Usage analytics are off; you can turn
  them on in Settings if you want to share anonymous usage numbers.)

  Jarvis only runs while your PC is on and you are signed in.
  To turn off starting at sign-in: open the Windows "Task Scheduler" app,
  click "Task Scheduler Library", right-click "OpenJarvis", choose Disable.

  Jarvis can only see and change files inside your Desktop folder. It can't
  touch anything else on the PC.

"HEY JARVIS" VOICE BAR
  Say "Hey Jarvis" (plain "Jarvis" often works too). A small bar pops up at
  the bottom of the screen. Say what you want, e.g. "open Spotify",
  "open my Downloads folder", "what's the weather?". Jarvis answers out loud,
  and for a few seconds after you can just keep talking. While he speaks, the
  bar steps aside for a glowing golden orb; click the orb to stop him talking.
  Move the bar: press on it (not on its buttons or the typing box) and drag it
  anywhere; it remembers the spot. Double-click it to put it back.
  HOLD-TO-TALK (best in a noisy room): hold the RIGHT Ctrl key, say your
  request, let go. No "Jarvis" needed.
  You can also type in the bar's box and press Enter.
  Privacy: the wake word is detected on this PC only; nothing is sent
  anywhere until you say it.
  If the microphone doesn't react: Windows Settings > Privacy & security >
  Microphone > allow desktop apps. Earphones with a mic help in noisy rooms.

TALK IN THE CHAT WINDOW
  Settings (left sidebar) > Speech > switch on "Speech-to-Text",
  "Text-to-Speech" and "Speak replies automatically". Then use the microphone
  button in the message box. The first spoken reply after installing can take
  a few minutes (the voice downloads once, about 1 GB).
  While Jarvis speaks, a glowing golden orb fills the chat (the text and message
  box come back when he finishes). Click anywhere or press Esc to stop him.

GMAIL + GOOGLE CALENDAR (optional, one time, about 5 minutes, read-only)
  1. App password: open https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and sign in.
     (If it isn't available, first turn on 2-Step Verification.)
     Type the name Jarvis, click Create, keep the 16-letter password on screen.
  2. Calendar link: https://calendar.google.com > gear icon > Settings > your
     calendar (left) > "Integrate calendar" > "Secret address in iCal format".
  3. Saving them: copy ONE thing, then double-click the shortcut
     "Save Google setting (copy it first)" in this JARVIS folder. Do it three
     times: the calendar secret address, the 16-letter app password, and your
     Gmail address. A message tells you what was saved.
  Keep these private: anyone with them can read your mail.

THE BRAIN (NVIDIA)
  Your free NVIDIA key is in:  {{ROOT}}\NVIDIA-API-KEY.txt
  To change it: open that file in Notepad, replace the key, press Ctrl+S,
  then double-click "Restart Jarvis". Get a key at https://build.nvidia.com
  You can switch the AI model from the menu at the top-left of the chat.
  Jarvis needs the internet for its brain.

SETTINGS (city, etc.)
  {{ROOT}}\JARVIS-SETTINGS.txt  - change, save, then "Restart Jarvis".

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Double-click "Restart Jarvis". If it can't start, a message says why.
  Logs: {{ROOT}}\logs
  "Stop Jarvis" turns it off. "Uninstall Jarvis" removes it.

PERSONALITY
  Jarvis has a dry, light sense of humour. Say "be serious" in a chat to
  switch it off for that conversation. To change it for good, edit
  {{HOME}}\SOUL.md in Notepad, save, then "Restart Jarvis".
