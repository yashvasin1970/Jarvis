JARVIS - SETUP KIT
==================

Jarvis is a personal AI assistant for your Windows PC. You can chat with it
or talk to it ("Hey Jarvis"), and it can search the web, make pictures, set
reminders, read and edit files on your Desktop, look at your screen, control
volume and brightness, summarise web pages and YouTube videos, and (if you
set it up) read your Gmail and Google Calendar.

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), about 12 GB of free disk space, and internet.
  - A free NVIDIA account for Jarvis's "brain" (the setup shows you how).
  - About 15-40 minutes. Most of it is downloading; you can do other things.

HOW TO INSTALL
  1. If you got a zip file: right-click it > "Extract All" > "Extract".
  2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Jarvis".
       If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info",
       then "Run anyway". If it asks "Do you want to run this file?": click Run.
  3. A window shows the progress. Type your city when asked (for weather).
  4. Near the end, an NVIDIA web page and Notepad open:
       - sign in to NVIDIA (free) and click "Generate API Key",
       - copy the key (it starts with nvapi-),
       - paste it into Notepad, press Ctrl+S to save, close Notepad,
       - press Enter in the setup window.
  5. Jarvis opens. Done! The guide "How to use Jarvis" is in the new JARVIS
     folder on your Desktop.

  If setup stops with a message, fix what it says (usually the internet
  connection) and double-click "Install Jarvis" again: it carries on.

PRIVACY
  - Jarvis runs on your PC; your chats, memories and settings stay on it.
  - What you ask is sent to NVIDIA's AI to be answered (a screenshot or an
    email only when you ask about it).
  - Jarvis can only see and change files in your Desktop folder.
  - This kit contains nothing from the person who sent it to you: no keys,
    passwords, chats or settings.

REMOVING IT
  Double-click "Uninstall Jarvis" in the JARVIS folder on your Desktop.
